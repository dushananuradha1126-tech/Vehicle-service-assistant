BRAKE SYSTEM MAINTENANCE & DIAGNOSTICS MANUAL

1. Brake Fluid Specifications
- DOT 3 / DOT 4 Fluid: Replace every 2 years or 20,000 km.
- Hygroscopic Property: Brake fluid absorbs atmospheric moisture over time, lowering boiling point and causing spongy pedal feel.

2. Brake Pad Inspection
- Safe Minimum Thickness: 3.0 mm. Replace pads immediately if below 2.0 mm.
- Squealing Sound: Indicates wear indicator tab contacting brake rotor disc.
- Grinding Noise: Severe metal-on-metal wear damaging rotors.

3. ABS System Diagnostics
- Spongy Pedal: Indicates air trapped in hydraulic lines (bleed required).
- Vehicle Pulls to Side: Seized caliper piston or uneven pad wear.
- ABS Light: Faulty wheel speed sensor or low fluid reservoir level.
