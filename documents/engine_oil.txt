ENGINE OIL & LUBRICATION MANUAL

1. Overview & Purpose
Engine oil is critical for lubricating moving engine components, cooling heat-prone areas, preventing friction wear, and sealing piston rings.

2. Change Intervals & Recommended Grades
- Synthetic Oil: Every 7,500 - 10,000 km (or 12 months)
- Semi-Synthetic Oil: Every 5,000 - 7,500 km
- Mineral Oil: Every 3,000 - 5,000 km
- Filter Replacement: Always replace oil filter with every oil change.

3. Symptoms of Low / Degraded Engine Oil
- Metallic knocking or ticking noise from valve train.
- Low oil pressure warning light (Red oil can symbol).
- Dark black texture with burnt odor on dipstick.
- Overheating engine during normal operation.

4. Inspection Procedure
- Park vehicle on level surface and let engine cool down for 5-10 minutes.
- Pull out dipstick, wipe clean, re-insert fully, and check oil level between MIN and MAX marks.
