EMERGENCY BREAKDOWN GUIDE

If the Motorcycle Stops Suddenly

Step 1
Move the motorcycle to a safe location.

Step 2
Turn on hazard lights if available.

Step 3
Check fuel level.

Step 4
Inspect battery connections.

Step 5
Check engine warning lights.

Step 6
Call roadside assistance if the motorcycle cannot be restarted safely.

Safety
Never attempt major roadside repairs on busy roads.
