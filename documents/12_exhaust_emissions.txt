EXHAUST & EMISSIONS CONTROL GUIDE

1. Catalytic Converter & Oxygen Sensors
- Upstream and downstream O2 sensors monitor air-fuel ratio and converter efficiency.
- Expected O2 sensor lifespan: 100,000 km.

2. Exhaust Leak & Emission Symptoms
- Loud sputtering sound under acceleration.
- Exhaust odor inside vehicle cabin (CO hazard).
- Check engine light code P0420 (Catalytic converter efficiency below threshold).
- Rotten egg sulfur smell indicating rich fuel mixture or failed converter.
