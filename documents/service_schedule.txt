VEHICLE SERVICE SCHEDULE & MILEAGE CHECKLIST

1. 5,000 km / 6 Months Service:
- Change engine oil and replace oil filter.
- Inspect tire tread depth and adjust air pressure (PSI).
- Inspect fluid levels (windshield washer, brake fluid, coolant).
- Check exterior lighting, turn signals, and horn.

2. 10,000 km / 12 Months Service:
- Rotate tires for even tread wear.
- Replace engine air filter and cabin air filter.
- Inspect front and rear brake pads and rotors.
- Lubricate door hinges, latches, and suspension points.

3. 20,000 km / 2 Years Service:
- Replace spark plugs (standard spark plugs).
- Flush and replace brake fluid.
- Inspect suspension bushings, ball joints, and tie rod ends.
- Test battery state of health and alternator charging voltage.

4. 40,000 km / 4 Years Service:
- Flush engine coolant / radiator fluid.
- Service transmission fluid and differential fluid.
- Inspect drive belts and replace serpentine belt if cracked.
- Clean fuel injectors and throttle body.
