BATTERY & ELECTRICAL DIAGNOSTICS MANUAL

1. Battery Voltage Standards
- Rest Voltage (Engine OFF): 12.6V to 12.8V (Fully Charged).
- Crank Voltage (Engine Starting): Must remain above 9.6V.
- Charging Voltage (Engine Running): 13.8V to 14.5V from alternator.

2. Symptoms of Weak Battery / Alternator Failure
- Slow starter motor crank on key turn.
- Rapid clicking sound from starter relay.
- Headlights dimming at idle speed.
- Red battery warning light on instrument cluster.

3. Maintenance Guidelines
- Clean battery posts with baking soda and wire brush to clear corrosion.
- Average battery lifespan: 3 to 5 years.
