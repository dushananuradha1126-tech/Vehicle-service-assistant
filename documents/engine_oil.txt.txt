ENGINE OIL MAINTENANCE GUIDE

Purpose
Engine oil lubricates moving engine components, reduces friction, removes heat, and protects the engine from wear.

Recommended Oil Change Interval
- Normal riding: Every 4,000 to 5,000 kilometers.
- Heavy traffic or dusty conditions: Every 3,000 kilometers.
- Long-distance riding: Follow the manufacturer's maintenance schedule.

Oil Level Inspection
- Check the engine oil level before long trips.
- Keep the motorcycle on level ground while checking.
- Never exceed the maximum oil level.

Signs That Engine Oil Needs Replacement
- Dark or dirty oil.
- Increased engine noise.
- Poor engine performance.
- Oil warning light appears.
- Difficult gear shifting.

Recommendations
- Always use the oil grade recommended by the manufacturer.
- Replace the oil filter whenever the engine oil is replaced.
- Never mix different oil grades unless approved by the manufacturer.
