AIR FILTRATION & CABIN HYGIENE GUIDE

1. Engine Air Filter
- Inspection Interval: Every 10,000 km. Replace every 15,000 to 20,000 km.
- Symptoms of Dirty Air Filter: Reduced horsepower, black exhaust smoke, poor acceleration.

2. Cabin HEPA Air Filter
- Replace every 12,000 to 15,000 km.
- Symptoms of Clogged Cabin Filter: Musty odor when AC is turned on, reduced climate control airflow, foggy windshield.
