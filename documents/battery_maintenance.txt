BATTERY & ELECTRICAL DIAGNOSTICS GUIDE

1. Battery Voltage Standards
- Rest Voltage (Engine OFF): 12.6V to 12.8V (100% state of charge).
- Crank Voltage (Starting): Should not drop below 9.6V.
- Charging Voltage (Engine Running @ 2000 RPM): 13.8V to 14.5V (Alternator output).

2. Symptoms of Weak Battery / Alternator Failure
- Slow engine crank when turning ignition key.
- Clicking sound from starter solenoid without engine turn over.
- Dimming headlights at idle that brighten when accelerating.
- Battery warning light (Red battery symbol) illuminated while driving.

3. Maintenance Recommendations
- Clean battery terminals with baking soda solution to remove blue/white corrosion.
- Ensure terminal clamps are tightly torqued.
- Average battery lifespan: 3 to 5 years.
