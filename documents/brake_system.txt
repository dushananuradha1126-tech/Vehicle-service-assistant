BRAKE SYSTEM MAINTENANCE & DIAGNOSTICS GUIDE

1. Brake Fluid Standards & Intervals
- Dot 3 / Dot 4 Fluid: Flush every 2 years or 20,000 km.
- Moisture Absorption: Brake fluid is hygroscopic and absorbs water, leading to spongy pedal feel and reduced stopping power.

2. Brake Pad Inspection
- Minimum Safe Thickness: 3 mm. Replace pads immediately if below 2 mm.
- Squealing Noise: Indicates wear indicator tab touching rotor.
- Grinding Noise: Metal-on-metal wear. Rotors must be resurfaced or replaced.

3. ABS & Hydraulic Symptoms
- Spongy Brake Pedal: Air bubbles in hydraulic lines requiring brake bleeding.
- Vehicle Pulls to One Side: Seized caliper piston or uneven pad wear.
- ABS Warning Light: Faulty wheel speed sensor, pump relay, or low fluid level.
