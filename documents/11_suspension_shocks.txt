SUSPENSION & SHOCK ABSORBER SERVICE GUIDE

1. Component Check
- Inspect struts, shock absorbers, sway bar end links, control arm bushings, and ball joints every 20,000 km.

2. Symptoms of Worn Suspension
- Excessive bouncing after hitting bump or dip.
- Nose-diving during hard braking.
- Hydraulic oil leaking down shock absorber body.
- Clunking sound over rough roads (indicates worn sway bar links or ball joints).
