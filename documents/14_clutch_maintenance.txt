MANUAL CLUTCH SYSTEM DIAGNOSTICS MANUAL

1. Clutch Components
- Friction disc, pressure plate, throwout bearing, flywheel, and hydraulic slave cylinder.

2. Symptoms of Clutch Wear
- Clutch slipping (Engine RPM increases under acceleration without vehicle speeding up).
- Burning friction odor after climbing steep hills.
- High pedal engagement point or hard gear shifting.
- Grinding noise when depressing clutch pedal (worn throwout bearing).
