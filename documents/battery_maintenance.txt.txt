BATTERY MAINTENANCE GUIDE

Battery Life
Most motorcycle batteries last between 2 and 4 years.

Inspection
- Check battery terminals monthly.
- Keep terminals clean.
- Remove corrosion immediately.
- Ensure battery cables are securely connected.

Charging
- Recharge immediately if battery voltage becomes low.
- Avoid completely discharging the battery.

Warning Signs
- Slow engine cranking.
- Dim headlights.
- Battery warning light.
- Difficulty starting the motorcycle.

Recommendations
Replace the battery if it fails to hold a proper charge.
