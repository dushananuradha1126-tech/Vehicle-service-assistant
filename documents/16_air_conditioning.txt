AUTOMOTIVE AIR CONDITIONING & CLIMATE CONTROL MANUAL

1. Refrigerant Standards
- R134a / R1234yf Refrigerant: Check pressure levels every 2 years.

2. Symptoms of Climate Control Faults
- AC blowing warm air (Low refrigerant, leaky condenser, or faulty compressor clutch).
- Clicking sound from engine bay as AC clutch constantly cycles on and off.
- Water leaking onto passenger carpet (Clogged AC evaporator drain tube).
