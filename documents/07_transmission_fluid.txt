TRANSMISSION FLUID SERVICE GUIDE

1. Automatic vs Manual Transmission Fluid
- ATF (Automatic Transmission Fluid): Flush every 40,000 to 60,000 km.
- Manual Gear Oil (75W-90): Replace every 50,000 to 80,000 km.
- CVT (Continuously Variable Transmission): Requires specialized CVT fluid every 40,000 km.

2. Warning Signs of Low or Burnt Transmission Fluid
- Delayed or harsh gear engagement.
- Slipping transmission during acceleration.
- Burning smell from engine bay.
- Dark brown or black fluid color (Fresh ATF is bright red).
