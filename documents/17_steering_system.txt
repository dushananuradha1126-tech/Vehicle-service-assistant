POWER STEERING & RACK AND PINION GUIDE

1. Power Steering Fluid Flush
- Replace power steering fluid every 40,000 to 50,000 km.
- Electric Power Steering (EPS): Uses electric motor instead of hydraulic pump.

2. Diagnostic Symptoms
- Whining noise when turning steering wheel (Low hydraulic fluid or failing power steering pump).
- Heavy steering effort at low parking speeds.
- Fluid puddle underneath rack and pinion inner tie rod boots.
