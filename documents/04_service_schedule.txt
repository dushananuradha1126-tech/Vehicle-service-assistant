PERIODIC SERVICE SCHEDULE & CHECKLIST

1. 5,000 km Service
- Engine oil and oil filter replacement.
- Inspect tire tread depth and adjust inflation PSI.
- Inspect brake fluid, windshield washer, and coolant levels.

2. 10,000 km Service
- Tire rotation for uniform tread wear.
- Engine air filter and cabin HEPA filter replacement.
- Front and rear brake pad inspection.

3. 20,000 km Service
- Spark plug inspection and gap adjustment.
- Brake fluid flush and replacement.
- Suspension link and ball joint inspection.

4. 40,000 km Service
- Radiator coolant drain and refill.
- Transmission fluid service.
- Serpentine drive belt inspection.
