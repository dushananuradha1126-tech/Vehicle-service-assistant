FUEL INJECTION & PUMP SYSTEM GUIDE

1. Fuel Filter & Injectors
- Replace inline fuel filter every 30,000 to 40,000 km (if equipped).
- Clean fuel injectors every 40,000 km to remove carbon deposits.

2. Symptoms of Fuel Delivery Issues
- Fuel pump whine from rear fuel tank.
- Engine cranks but fails to start due to loss of fuel pressure.
- Stuttering or hesitation at high RPM or loaded acceleration.
