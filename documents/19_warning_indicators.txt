OBD-II DIAGNOSTIC CODES & DASHBOARD WARNING LIGHTS GUIDE

1. Dashboard Warning Symbols
- Red Oil Can: Critical low engine oil pressure. Stop engine immediately.
- Red Battery: Alternator charging system failure.
- Red Temperature Light: Engine overheating. Pull over safely.
- Amber Check Engine Light: Powertrain emission fault detected.

2. Common OBD-II Trouble Codes
- P0300: Random / Multiple cylinder misfire.
- P0171: System too lean (Air leak or dirty MAF sensor).
- P0420: Catalytic converter system efficiency below threshold.
- P0128: Coolant thermostat operating below regulating temperature.
