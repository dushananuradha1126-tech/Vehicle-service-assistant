WHEEL BEARING & HUB ASSEMBLY DIAGNOSTICS

1. Function & Design
- Sealed wheel bearings allow low-friction rotation of road wheels while supporting vehicle weight.

2. Failure Symptoms
- Humming, growling, or roaring noise that increases with vehicle speed.
- Noise gets louder when leaning into a turn.
- Play or looseness in wheel when tested on jack stands.
- ABS sensor warning light triggered by magnetic tone ring corruption.
