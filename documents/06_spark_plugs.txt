SPARK PLUGS & IGNITION SYSTEM GUIDE

1. Spark Plug Types & Intervals
- Standard Copper Plug: Replace every 20,000 to 30,000 km.
- Platinum Plug: Replace every 60,000 to 80,000 km.
- Iridium Plug: Replace every 100,000 km.

2. Diagnostic Symptoms of Faulty Spark Plugs
- Engine misfire (Rough idle and stuttering under acceleration).
- Increased fuel consumption.
- Hard starting, especially in cold weather.
- Check engine light flashing with P0300 misfire codes.
