VEHICLE WARRANTY POLICY

Warranty Period
Most manufacturers provide a warranty between 2 and 5 years depending on the model.

Warranty Covers
- Manufacturing defects.
- Engine defects.
- Transmission defects.
- Factory-installed components.

Warranty Does Not Cover
- Accidents.
- Unauthorized modifications.
- Improper maintenance.
- Normal wear and tear.

Recommendation
Always service the motorcycle according to the manufacturer's maintenance schedule to keep the warranty valid.
