VEHICLE WARRANTY & REPAIR COVERAGE POLICY MANUAL

1. Factory Warranty Terms
- Bumper-to-Bumper Warranty: Typically 3 years or 60,000 km.
- Powertrain Warranty: Covers engine, transmission, and drive axles for 5 years or 100,000 km.

2. Warranty Void Conditions
- Failure to perform scheduled engine oil changes according to maintenance guidelines.
- Unauthorized vehicle modifications or installing unapproved aftermarket performance tuning chips.
- Using incorrect fluid grades or driving through flooded roads causing engine hydro-lock.
