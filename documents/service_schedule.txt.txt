VEHICLE SERVICE SCHEDULE

1,000 km Service
- General inspection
- Tighten nuts and bolts
- Inspect brakes
- Check tire pressure
- Check chain adjustment

5,000 km Service
- Replace engine oil
- Replace oil filter
- Clean air filter
- Inspect battery
- Lubricate chain
- Check brake pads

10,000 km Service
- Replace spark plug
- Replace air filter if required
- Inspect suspension
- Check steering bearings
- Inspect cooling system

20,000 km Service
- Replace coolant
- Replace brake fluid
- Inspect clutch system
- Inspect fuel system
- Complete safety inspection
