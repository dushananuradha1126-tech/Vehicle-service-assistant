ENGINE OIL & LUBRICATION MANUAL

1. Overview & Purpose
Engine oil is critical for lubricating moving engine components, cooling heat-prone areas, preventing friction wear, and sealing piston rings.

2. Change Intervals & Recommended Grades
- Synthetic Oil: Every 7,500 - 10,000 km (or 12 months)
- Semi-Synthetic Oil: Every 5,000 - 7,500 km
- Mineral Oil: Every 3,000 - 5,000 km
- Filter Replacement: Always replace oil filter with every oil change.

3. Viscosity Ratings (SAE Standards)
- 5W-30: Common for modern gasoline engines in moderate temperatures.
- 0W-20: Fuel-efficient low-viscosity synthetic oil for modern engines.
- 10W-40: Used for motorcycles and high-mileage engines.

4. Symptoms of Low or Degraded Oil
- Metallic knocking noise from engine head.
- Oil pressure warning light illuminated on dashboard.
- Black, sludge-like oil texture on dipstick.
