TIRE & WHEEL ALIGNMENT MANUAL

1. Inflation Pressure (PSI)
- Check cold tire inflation pressure monthly.
- Typical passenger car range: 30 to 35 PSI.

2. Tread Depth & Wear Inspection
- Minimum Legal Tread Depth: 1.6 mm. Recommended replacement at 3.0 mm.
- Feathered Wear Pattern: Indicates incorrect toe alignment.
- Edge Wear: Indicates under-inflation or aggressive cornering.

3. Wheel Balancing & Alignment
- Perform wheel balancing every 10,000 km or when steering wheel vibrates above 80 km/h.
- Perform wheel alignment after replacing steering/suspension components or hitting potholes.
