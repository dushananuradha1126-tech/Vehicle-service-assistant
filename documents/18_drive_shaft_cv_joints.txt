DRIVE SHAFT & CV JOINT MAINTENANCE GUIDE

1. CV Axle & Boots
- Constant Velocity (CV) joints allow drive shafts to transmit power through variable angles while wheels steer and move up and down.

2. Symptoms of Failure
- Clicking or popping sound when making sharp turns (indicates worn outer CV joint).
- Heavy grease thrown onto inside of front wheel rim (indicates torn rubber CV boot).
- Vibration during heavy acceleration (worn inner tripod CV joint).
