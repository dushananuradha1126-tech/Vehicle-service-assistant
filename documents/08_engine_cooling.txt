ENGINE COOLING & RADIATOR SYSTEM GUIDE

1. Coolant Specifications
- Ethylene Glycol / OAT Coolant: Flush and refill every 40,000 km or 3 years.
- Mixing 50/50 coolant with distilled water prevents boiling and freezing.

2. Overheating Troubleshooting
- High Temperature Gauge Reading: Indicates stuck thermostat, failed water pump, low coolant, or radiator fan failure.
- Sweet Burnt Odor: Indicates coolant leak from hoses or head gasket.
- White Steam from Exhaust: Indicates blown head gasket leaking coolant into combustion chamber.
